> A zabloing is a piece of text, and can include links to websites, images, videos, etc.
> The zabloing command will only work in approved servers.
> Zabloings are *global* and can be seen by anyone in an approved server.

`zabloing (number)`: Get a zabloing
`zabloing-count`: Show the total number of zabloings
`zabloing-search (query)`: Search for a zabloing containing (query)
`zabloing-add`: Add a zabloing
